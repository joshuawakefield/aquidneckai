## Owner direction and conclusion

The October 7 clarification makes useful news from local resources and worthwhile authors/publications central for Island residents and SMBs. Recent exercise/connection work underrepresented this purpose. Preserve those paths and trades examples, but do not require every reader to adopt a trades persona. Two complementary streams—local developments and broader ideas with substantive local usefulness—should lead the proposed home. Owner confidence in a publication is a preference, not verification of its every claim.

Reuse the existing source adapters, registry, evidence, editorial review and bounded feed. The main gaps are stage visibility, individual-story extraction from page watches, provenance display, and selection policy. More endpoints or a new crawler would not resolve these gaps. The current classifier also rejects ordinary useful local news without explicit AI evidence; broader useful local coverage is a proposed editorial-policy change, not implemented behavior.

## Proposed selection and smallest architecture change

Select a small useful edition, not a generic breaking-news firehose. Local developments can earn selection through substantive community usefulness without fabricated AI wording; broader AI/technology ideas must have a credible reader use case without pretending they originated locally. This extends proposed product scope, but current AI-only assessment and narrow automatic publication stay unchanged pending separate fixture-led policy review.

Before selection require: accessible original item; accurate publisher/byline (unknown if absent); a concise development or clearly labeled evergreen resource; source date or unknown; actual check time and scope; exact supporting evidence privately retained; local fact distinct from editorial interpretation; commercial disclosure or unknown; rights/access constraints; uncertainties and any expiry/correction. Next actions are optional and supported, never invented. Original reporting, institutional/maker claims, commentary, research and directory/digest leads need distinct labels. No popularity, sponsorship, “honesty” preference or AI score substitutes for these gates.

Reuse existing observation/source/entry relationships. Propose a small allowlisted reader DTO, initially fixtures: `publication`, `author`, `sourcePublishedAt`, `sourceCheckedAt`, `checkScope`, `addedToAqaiAt`, `stream`, `evidenceKind`, `localInterpretation`, `nextAction`, `commercialDisclosure`, `uncertainty`, existing canonical URL/status/event dates. These are proposed semantics, not a new schema or a promise all values exist. Registry organization and observation source date/time can provide some data; an observed_at fetch timestamp must be labeled collected unless an editorial check and scope exist. Author is discarded by current normalization and cannot be recovered by guessing. Keep existing source link until a true detail route is justified.
