# Controlled source rollout

Updated 2026-10-06. Current counts and boundaries are in [PROJECT-STATE](PROJECT-STATE.md); endpoint inventories and cadence are in the [recurring-source report](reports/2026-10-05-recurring-sources.md) and [source expansion](reports/2026-10-05-source-expansion.md).

An enabled flag records intent; it does not prove a working collector. Distinguish registered, verified, scheduled, healthy, assessed and published. At the last check 208 of 269 endpoints were scheduled and 34 of 39 RI municipalities had scheduled coverage. This is not a claim to read every department, document or social channel.

For a user-authorized bounded expansion:

1. Find an official/public feed, API or usable page and establish exact scope.
2. Validate URL rules, bounded downloads, dates, stable identities, parser behavior and deduplication with fixtures.
3. Deploy the tested adapter to staging before activating dependent registry entries.
4. Preserve source identity, prior definition and verification evidence; activate the reviewed group only.
5. Verify recurring runs, released leases, unchanged-content handling, assessment completion, cost and visible failures.
6. Keep collection permission separate from editorial publication. Unsupported widget/portal shells and blocked sources remain honest gaps.

The registry is explicit; no unattended system should widen its source list on its own. Authenticated add/edit/disable controls are AQ-006. Five blocked municipalities are AQ-009; current Scituate/RTX failures are AQ-004. Do not bypass access challenges or hide failures.

Public launch remains separate. Use AQ-010 to prepare reader/admin separation, useful content, health/alert behavior, rollback and DNS/mail preservation. Endpoint count alone is not launch readiness.
